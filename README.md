## What is Costeo Adobe?

Costeo Adobe delivers an interactive environment tailored for assembling Adobe Experience Cloud architecture plans and business cases. By centralizing practical use case reviews, licensing frameworks, bid optimizations, and budget calculations, it standardizes how solution teams frame their proposals.

Instead of navigating fragmented spreadsheets and documents, teams can instantly map business requirements to precise licensing parameters and expected software costs, establishing a solid baseline for enterprise Adobe initiatives.
